# Project kickoff: youth music, learning, and practical next steps

## Suggested long-term goal

Develop, youth-validate, and maintain a free, credible, music-first resource for aspiring young rappers, initially ages 13–18 in the United States, especially young people who themselves express the belief that a rap career makes school unnecessary. Show the learning behind musical craft through carefully sourced artist process stories and useful original activities, then help visitors choose realistic educational actions and connect with reliable human support. Respect rap ambition, agency, language varieties, and unconventional educational paths. Use modest, testable claims; establish usefulness before expanding or claiming educational impact.

## Principles to preserve

- Sister’s juvenile-defenders-office observations are anecdotal inspiration, not prevalence evidence or a causal finding. Audience eligibility follows an expressed belief/need, not race or justice status.
- Learning and practice can support music and preserve options; a particular credential is not mandatory or sufficient for music success. Grades are not artistic ability or intelligence.
- Every story should reveal something a visitor can try: story → observed practice → short activity → optional educational connection → realistic next action.
- Respect African American English and other language varieties. Expand repertoire and deliberate choice; do not “correct” identity or equate rare words with intelligence.
- Include counterexamples and interrupted schooling honestly. Do not romanticize dropout or hide it to force the argument.
- Routes should address writing, credit recovery, enrollment/support, music business, a trusted adult, or uncertainty. Motivation alone does not solve transportation, safety, disability services, immediate income, missing credits or other barriers.
- First outcomes: respect, relevance, comprehension, usability, negative reactions, voluntary next-step selection, and optional consented follow-through. Intention is not behavior; behavior is not attendance/graduation/justice impact.
- Use original teaching bars and explicitly fictional business scenarios. Do not reproduce full lyrics, assume educational fair use, imply artist endorsement, or reuse minors’ work/testimonials without a separate appropriate process.
