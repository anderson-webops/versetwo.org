Your next step

What would help right now?

Pick something useful, or leave it for another time. You do not have to explain your personal history.

Get better at writing

Choose one thing: a clearer scene, stronger delivery or useful feedback.

“Can you look at these two versions and tell me where the idea gets clearer?”

Try a teacher, tutor, librarian or a trusted person in an established arts program.

Check a fact or reference

Choose one claim you want to use in music or an assignment.

“Does this source support what I wrote, and where could I check it?”

Try a librarian or subject teacher. Use the provided offline source cards if browsing is not available.

Explain a creative choice

Compare two arrangements, placements or pauses.

“This is what I want someone to notice. What changes between these versions?”

Try a music or drama teacher, mentor or willing listener in an established arts program. Ask before sharing; you choose what to keep.

Catch up or check my credits

Ask a school counselor or education support worker about one missing assignment, credit or enrollment question.

“What is one realistic step, and who can help me take it?”

If you are not enrolled, ask about locally approved options and eligibility. You do not need to decide on a pathway from this page.

Understand music business

Start with a budget or write questions about an invented offer.

“What will I actually receive, what work is expected, and who controls the recording?”

For real agreements, involve a trusted adult and qualified advice. Do not share a contract or personal information with strangers online.

School is not working for me

Safety, transport, learning support, work and missing credits need practical help.

“This is the part I need help with. What can we change first?”

Choose a reliable counselor, teacher, caregiver, youth advocate or education worker you already know or can meet through a trusted organization.

I need money now

A future plan does not pay today's bill. Ask a trusted adult or youth-program staff member about age-appropriate work and immediate support, alongside education options.

You decide how much to explain. This site cannot provide a job, case management or financial support.

I am not sure yet

You can try one activity without making a plan. You can also choose no next step right now.

When you want to, start with: “I want music to help me do ____. One thing I want to understand is ____.”

Make the request concrete

If you choose an action, decide what you will ask, who you can ask and when it is realistic. A support person should follow their organization's safeguarding rules. You can stop, change the plan or choose another person.
