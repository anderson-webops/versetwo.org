Music / Practice - local review copy

1. Extract the ZIP first. Keep all extracted files in the same folder.
2. Open index.html in your usual browser. No installation or account is needed.
3. Stories, activities and next-step prompts use local files. External source
   websites require internet and may have their own ads or tracking.
4. Open music-practice-packet.pdf for the 23-page printable version.
5. Open facilitator-outline.pdf for a one-page adult session outline.
6. Activities happen on paper, in your own notes, through speech or reflection.
   The website does not receive or save your answers.

To check offline use, close the browser tab, disconnect your device from the
internet in your usual way, then reopen index.html and navigate between pages.
Do not change security protections or permissions to make it open. If local
HTML opening is blocked by your organization, use the included PDF instead.

This is a review prototype, not a youth-tested program. The name remains open.
Artists have not endorsed the project. The adult guide explains the limits of
use and requirements before any evaluation with minors.
