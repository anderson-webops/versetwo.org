## The governing original research brief

The exact wording is preserved at source-packet/original-research-brief.md. This summary must not replace it. Its eight research tasks are:

1. Stress-test the central argument; distinguish school, grades, completion, college, independent learning, mentorship, practice and musical skill; include counterexamples.
2. Build a verified artist evidence collection, roughly 12–15 candidates and strongest six–eight if evidence supports them. Prefer process stories over credential lists; test relevance with intended users.
3. Map learning skills to actual musical/professional tasks, with original activities, practical routes and limits. Respect AAE and other language varieties.
4. Find a few defensible statistics with population, denominator, timeframe, method and exclusions. No invented success odds, celebrity wealth estimates or graduation percentages from selected artists.
5. Review original studies and high-quality syntheses, including contrary evidence and transfer limits. Program publicity and participant testimony are not causal proof.
6. Build a small accessible website plan: mission, naming, sitemap, profile template, verified samples, five original activities, seven-day plan, facilitator outline and respectful answers to objections.
7. Propose a small ethical pilot with qualified partners, criticism that can falsify the premise, noncoercion, minimal data and trusted human support.
8. Apply strict sourcing, copyright, permissions and endorsement standards. Direct URLs/dates and verified locations; label inaccessible/unverified material.

The requested report output is also eight parts: executive recommendation/refined concept; artist collection; statistics/methods; research/implications; skill map; site/content; pilot/evaluation; claims to avoid/unresolved questions. docs/requirements-reconciliation.md maps the original requirements to delivered and remaining work.

## Evidence limits the next chat must preserve

Imported reports are research inputs, not blanket independent verification. Only individually checked claims belong on learner pages. Interviews support accurate attribution; educational connections and activities are our teaching choices. Historical habits must not become current daily habits. Enrollment, graduation, honorary degrees and university appointments remain distinct.

- Megan's profile uses her 2020 studies; an institutional graduation record was not inspected. Tierra Whack notes/voice/selection leads, new Megan/Doechii markers and other unchecked anecdotes remain excluded.
- [Canning and Harackiewicz (2015)](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED565393.pdf) concerns undergraduate mental-math experiments. Study 1 direct messaging did not significantly harm interest/performance versus neutral control; comparison with self-generated relevance and baseline confidence matters. It did not measure psychological reactance.
- [Van der Beek, Bellhäuser and Hertel](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/1475725720965002) measured distinct voluntary online units opened, not completion/learning. Its analyzed sample excluded writing noncompliers; do not call it intention-to-treat or a universal null effect.
- [Gaspard and colleagues' author manuscript](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/355069985_The_potential_of_relevance_interventions_for_scaling_up_A_cluster-randomized_trial_testing_the_effectiveness_of_a_relevance_intervention_in_math_classrooms) reported increased total cost; cost hypotheses were not preregistered and facet analyses were exploratory. Reactance was proposed, not measured. Do not treat all cost facets as significantly increased.
- Adjapong/Emdin 2015's 31 questionnaire respondents, Evans 2019's 30 participants and Evans 2021/2023's 57 are separate studies. Do not merge or sum them to repair an imported label.
- Kelly 2020's exact publisher abstract matched, but full methods/sample/instruments/analysis/author limitations remain inaccessible. Kelly/Sawyer 2019 is another source. No access bypass occurred.
- Broad musician wage statistics exclude groups including self-employed workers and are not rapper income or success odds. Numeric earnings stay off learner pages.
- The first-report evidence-register attachment bodies and generated image were not captured. The complete research export is preserved, but these gaps are real. No reconstructed original attachment or complete chat export is claimed.

No evidence establishes the website's effects on attendance, graduation or justice outcomes, or the prevalence of the target belief. Artist popularity, naming, reading difficulty and usefulness need intended-user review. The pilot uses original, unvalidated formative prompts; it separates goal affirmation, usefulness, same-step confidence, offered/declined/opened/started/completed/stopped activity, voluntary next-step selection/attempt, pressure, burden and barriers. Keep decliners/missing responses visible. Changed steps cannot be treated as matched confidence change. Interest, intention, action and learning are different.
