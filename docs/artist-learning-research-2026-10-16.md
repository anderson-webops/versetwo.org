## Practical connections, proposed by this project

| Story | Musical choice | Optional practice / learning route |
|---|---|---|
| Eminem | Word meaning and sound | New “One word. More choices.” activity; own notes, a dictionary, library help or a language teacher. |
| Rakim | Timing, pauses and phrasing | Existing emphasis activity; private tapping, marked pauses, music or drama support. |
| Lupe Fiasco | One technique and a precise feedback question | New word activity; independent comparison or a willing writing group, teacher or mentor. |
| Doechii | Preparation and delivery | Existing emphasis activity; a short rehearsal or a silent comparison of placements. |

These are our teaching choices, not documented artist exercises. The new example uses ordinary words and invented sentences. No lyrics, recordings, photographs or trademarks were copied. Sound, meaning, performance, simplicity and a listener's response can all matter; vocabulary size is not an intelligence or artistic-quality score.

The earlier Lupe process-source gap is now closed for LF1/LF2 through the Johns Hopkins interview. MIT's separate department course URL still returned an error in this pass; it is not needed to support the new story. No new MIT credential or current-appointment claim is made.

Doechii's education/rehearsal story is now checked through two original interviews. This does not blanket-validate the imported report's separate song-incubation anecdote or other unresolved citation markers. Megan's degree-completion gap and the other old hold items remain outside this increment.

The expanded ten-story collection is a review selection, not a claim about current teenagers' recognition or preferences. Intended-user usefulness, reading difficulty and musical relevance remain open. The activity allows “no connection,” adaptation, silent participation and stopping; human support is optional. No website educational outcomes are established by these artist accounts.
