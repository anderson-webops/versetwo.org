## One-page brief

**Working project:** youth music and learning resource; public name unselected.

**Intended users:** initially US ages 13–18 who express an interest in rap and, particularly, the belief that a rap career makes school unnecessary. Anecdotal origin is not prevalence evidence. No demographic or justice-status eligibility assumption.

**Experience:** a documented artist practice, one original short activity, and an optional realistic learning/support step. Current local prototype: six process stories, six activities and a flexible music/learning week, sources, need-based support prompts and a printable adult guide. No accounts, uploads, tracking or mandatory performance. Different language varieties and formal/informal pathways respected. No artist or institutional endorsement implied.

**First request:** an adult staff reviewer or teaching artist critiques one page and one activity for musical usefulness, respectful framing and feasibility. Ask what should change or whether the premise is wrong. This is not a request for promotion, endorsement, youth access or a funding commitment.

**What Jacob contributes:** a free usable activity, source ledger, editable local files, print materials and a documented response to critique. Staff time, youth advice and accessibility work should be budgeted, not assumed free. No existing funding or partner agreement.

**Later possibility:** co-design and a small voluntary formative pilot only after a qualified host resolves safeguarding, review, permission/assent/consent, privacy, access needs and human support. Initial questions: respect, relevance, comprehension, activity usability, negative reactions and voluntary next-step selection. No claim of improving attendance, graduation or justice outcomes.

**Review decision:** Is the activity useful enough to revise and try in an existing appropriate setting? Can a participant decline and still receive every usual service? Can support staff address an actual barrier? A partner choosing to use it again would be a useful later milestone, not proof of impact.
