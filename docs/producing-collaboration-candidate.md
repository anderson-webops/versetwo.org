## What the learner can do

Create or use four original phrases/patterns, choose one part to repeat, compare its placement and explain a choice. The worked example uses only our invented phrases. A willing collaborator can suggest an alternative; solo, written and silent responses work. No equipment, hearing task, recording or upload is required. The activity links to a concrete teacher/mentor feedback request and the flexible week plan.

This is our teaching proposal, not the artists' method or an evaluated intervention. No recording, image, lyric or source embed was copied. The source page may contain mature personal material and remains optional.
