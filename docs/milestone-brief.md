## Research qualification

[Canning and Harackiewicz (2015)](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED565393.pdf) studied undergraduate mental-math tasks; its direct-message arm did not significantly harm performance or interest versus neutral control in Study 1. Comparisons with self-generated relevance and confidence matter. [Van der Beek, Bellhäuser and Hertel](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/1475725720965002) measured distinct online units opened, not completion or learning; its analyzed sample excluded writing noncompliers. [Gaspard and colleagues' author manuscript](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/355069985_The_potential_of_relevance_interventions_for_scaling_up_A_cluster-randomized_trial_testing_the_effectiveness_of_a_relevance_intervention_in_math_classrooms) reported increased total cost, with non-preregistered cost hypotheses and exploratory facets. These studies did not measure psychological reactance. They inform cautious design questions, not this website's proven effects.

Evans 2019's 30 participants, Adjapong/Emdin 2015's 31 questionnaire respondents and Evans 2021/2023's 57 form separate studies. The new report's unresolved label cannot be repaired by combining them. Its unavailable Rapsody source does not invalidate the project's previously checked 2014 original interview.
