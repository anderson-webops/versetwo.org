# Product, editorial and ethical brief

Review draft · October 2, 2026 · Jacob's youth music and learning project

## Purpose and audience

A free resource where an aspiring young rapper can examine a documented practice, try something privately and choose useful learning or human support. Initial design assumption: US ages 13–18, particularly people who themselves say that a rap career makes school unnecessary. Race, income and justice status do not establish that belief or eligibility. The sister's observations are anecdotal inspiration; prevalence is unknown. Center Black artists and include women without manufacturing a racial or class aesthetic.

Music and learning are explicit purposes. Grades are not artistic talent, intelligence or moral worth. Formal school, independent reading, mentors, libraries, training and community can all contribute. No particular credential, pathway, income or fame is promised. The prototype has not been validated with young people and has no demonstrated school outcomes.

## First experience

Home offers a writing activity immediately, with stories as another entry. Story → documented practice → original demonstration → private activity → voluntary education/support action. Visitors can skip stories, decline action, use an invented character or work without recording. Prototype label: Music / Practice, a descriptive temporary label rather than a proposed cleared brand.

Six process stories: Black Thought (drafting and interrupted college), Rapsody (feedback and delivery), Megan Thee Stallion (historical study alongside music), GZA (curiosity as writing material), Erykah Badu/J Dilla (a recalled production lesson), Little Simz (returning to an earlier draft). Selection reflects accessible evidence; intended-user recognition is untested. The Megan story deliberately uses her 2020 account rather than an unverified institutional graduation claim. No artist likenesses, lyrics, logos or recordings are required. Six implemented original activities: revision, an invented show budget, emphasis, checking a factual reference fictional viewpoints and arranging an original phrase/pattern. A flexible week page combines music practice with voluntary learning/support actions. The source cards work offline; activities and plan remain untested teaching proposals.

Sitemap: Home → Stories → Practice → Your week → Next step. Secondary: Sources, For adults, Print packet, About (included with Sources). Story template: brief factual paragraph; linked interview and date; observable practice; interpretation and limitation; short prompt; learning connection; private next step. One source is adequate when it is a directly inspected original interview; repeated summaries are not independent corroboration.

## Editorial controls

1. Every publishable fact needs a ledger ID, direct accessible source, publisher/date, event timeframe, source type, inspected location, paraphrase, confidence/basis, interpretation and limitation. Use a historical tense where appropriate. No invented timestamps.
2. Institutional confirmation, self-report, program description, independent reporting and our teaching choices remain distinct. Source checking does not establish independent truth of autobiographical recollections or endorsement.
3. Exclude daily dictionary claims, invented linguistics credentials, academic honors without records, celebrity net worth, success odds and graduation rates calculated from selected artists. Enrollment, honorary degrees, namesake fellowships and visiting appointments are not earned credentials.
4. Original activities do not imitate artists' lyrics or copy full verses. Word choice, imagery, simplicity, timing, melody, narrative and silence can all serve a piece. Respect AAE and other language varieties; feedback concerns intended meaning and audience, not dialect correction.
5. Stories of interrupted schooling stay visible. They neither show that leaving school causes success nor require everyone to follow a celebrity's path. School support should address actual barriers, including credits, learning access, safety, transportation and immediate income.
6. No logos or names imply affiliation. No photographs, music, testimonials or minors' creative work are reused by default. Any later third-party assets require documented permissions/reuse basis and appropriate review. Current prototype has none.
7. Corrections before publication: Jacob maintains ledger and dated changes; a reviewer flags the exact claim and source. Unresolved disputes cause removal or cautious rewrite. No public corrections contact is invented before an owner/contact is agreed.

## Presentation and access

Typography carries a restrained music-publication direction: deep ink, white surfaces and cobalt links, broad margins, numbered exercises and clearly separated drafts. No generated graffiti or slang. System fonts and plain HTML/CSS: no build, font download, scripts, media, trackers, forms, accounts or uploads. Content works by opening index.html offline; source links require internet but not activity completion. Printable HTML combines stories, activities, support and adult outline. Written, spoken, read-aloud and silent alternatives; no forced performance. Mobile reflow, visible keyboard focus, skip link and readable type. WCAG 2.2 AA is a design target, not a certification; manual checks and remaining limits recorded in validation.md. Future media needs captions and equivalent transcripts before inclusion.

## Ethical pilot boundary

This is an adult-review package, not recruitment authorization. A qualified host must determine appropriate safeguards, permission/assent/consent, review requirements, privacy limits, accessibility and human handoff before minors participate. Begin in a community setting; custodial testing requires separate experienced leadership. No participation consequences for grades, legal treatment, privileges or service access. No collection in this prototype. No cases, charges, exact dates of birth, addresses, trauma or creative work retained by default. Pilot notes, if subsequently authorized, should exclude identifying and unnecessary sensitive content; host controls contact information and retention.

## Research reconciliation

The original brief requests approximately 12–15 candidates and strongest six–eight; research proposes different slates. Neither is a verified relevance result. Both reports support process over credentials and a smaller first prototype. Their 8–15 versus 12–16 pilot counts are planning proposals, not power calculations; no count selected yet. Their different fictional $500 budgets are different scenarios, not factual contradictions. Prototype adopts the first scenario, preserving reserve versus expense distinction. Seven-day plans are optional teaching proposals, not outcome evidence or an enrollment instruction.

## October 3 — revised design hypothesis

A learner's own music goal → an authentic optional task → the learner's connection, including none → a chosen learning route → a voluntary attempt. This is a testable design proposal; no reviewed study tests the whole website pathway. Research claims/limits and primary links are in evidence-register.md (UV1–UV2,VB1–VB2,GP1–GP2). The task has artistic value without agreement about school.

Every activity now asks what it might help the learner make or understand before offering a route to adapt or skip. Practice starts with a privately chosen goal. Returning to an earlier draft is optional; it is not a productivity obligation. Examples may scaffold a choice but cannot prescribe its meaning. Independent practice, collaborators, libraries/community programs and formal education remain available routes.

Usefulness, confidence/attainability, voluntary action, pressure/reactance, burden and music-goal affirmation stay separate in pilot review. The original papers qualify the report's strongest harm/action claims; perceived usefulness or agreement cannot stand in for action, and high average ratings cannot erase negative responses. Measures are original formative prompts, not validated scales or an outcome trial.
