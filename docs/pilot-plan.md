# Formative pilot plan — qualified-partner review draft

Updated October 3, 2026. No recruitment, sessions or youth data collection authorized. No partner chosen. This is a review specification; the website has no collection widgets.

## Question and evidence basis

Can a learner try an authentic music task, identify their own useful connection (or none), and voluntarily attempt a feasible learning action while feeling their music goal and choices are respected? This design cannot establish attendance, graduation or justice impact.

Primary-source checks qualify the new report: [Canning and Harackiewicz's laboratory study](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED565393.pdf) informs optional self-generated connections; [van der Beek, Bellhäuser and Hertel's voluntary-training study](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/1475725720965002) informs the distinction between opening and doing; [Gaspard and colleagues' author manuscript](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/355069985_The_potential_of_relevance_interventions_for_scaling_up_A_cluster-randomized_trial_testing_the_effectiveness_of_a_relevance_intervention_in_math_classrooms) informs separate burden review. Exact designs, comparators, exclusions and findings are in evidence-register.md (UV1–UV2, VB1–VB2, GP1–GP2). These sources do not establish this website's effects. Proposed pressure questions below are our original, unvalidated prompts; they are not a validated reactance scale.

## Preparation before youth involvement

Host reviews intended audience and setting; determines appropriate review/permission/assent/consent; establishes safeguarding, confidentiality limits, accessible response formats, opt-out without consequences, compensation regardless of opinion, complaints route and reliable human support. Jacob and host agree responsibilities, data owner, retention, incident handling and budget. Adult critique precedes youth sessions. No custody-based first pilot without a separately qualified experienced host.

Start with a small co-design group; host chooses a practical formative count. Earlier 8–15/12–16 proposals are not power calculations. No effect-size estimate is sought. Include people who themselves identify with the music ambition and intended belief; avoid inference from race, income or justice status and do not solicit charges/cases as eligibility. Verify support needs individually, without inferring reading level from age. Shorten sessions to fit the participant.

## Suggested session, adjustable 25–35 minutes

1. Explain choice, actual privacy limits and right to stop. Ask what the homepage seems to offer without explaining the desired answer. If approved, name one chosen music step and briefly ask how manageable that same step feels before exposure.
2. Let participant choose a story, activity or neither. Ask what they want to make or understand; a private/fictional goal is enough. An artist is optional.
3. Offer the task and examples. The participant may skip, shorten, try, complete or stop. Do not require lyrics, writing disclosure, recording or performance. Do not count a click as completion.
4. Ask whether and how any practice helps their own goal; accept no connection. Only then discuss an optional route: independent practice, collaborator, library/community resource, class or trusted support person.
5. Offer one optional action after reflection. Record selection separately from an actual attempt, only if later permission/review allows notes. A missing support/resource route is a barrier, not a motivation failure.
6. Invite criticism, pressure/burden feedback and changes. Close with an available support route, without asking for praise or a school commitment.

## Minimal measures, if subsequently authorized

The host selects a short accessible set, prioritizing voluntary action, the learner's connection and negative reactions. For any before/after manageability question, name the same learner-chosen step before exposure. If the step changes, record the change separately and do not treat different-step ratings as improvement or decline. Do not turn the following menu into a long questionnaire. Every question may be declined; speaking, pointing, read-aloud or silent response is valid. No summed success score.

| Dimension | Minimal record | Original prompt/example | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chosen goal / confidence | Optional broad skill and perceived manageability before/after | “How manageable does this next music step feel?” | Not ability, intelligence or a validated efficacy measure |
| Self-generated connection | Own connection / no connection / skipped; category only | “Could this practice help something you want to make or understand? How, or does it not fit?” | Do not grade dialect, polish or agreement about school; no lyrics retained |
| Voluntary task action | Offered, declined, opened, started, completed, stopped; assistance level | Observe only what they consent to show | Opening, attempting, completing and learning are separate; no creative-work capture |
| Post-reflection action | None, selected, attempted in session, or blocked; broad route | “Is there one step you want to try? None is fine.” | Selection is intention; an attempt is not educational attainment |
| Choice / pressure | Optional de-identified category and reason | “Did this respect your choices? Did any part feel like it was pushing you toward an answer?” | Original feedback, not measured psychological reactance or a validated scale |
| Goal affirmation / hidden agenda | Optional reason, including uncertainty | “Did this take your music goal seriously? What do you think it wanted you to do?” | Do not infer agreement from politeness or completion |
| Burden / discouragement | Before/after manageability if approved; optional reason | “Did anything make the next step feel harder or like too much work?” | Report alongside usefulness; do not average away negative responses |
| Language respect / usability | Confusion, help needed, optional reason | “Did anything sound judgmental about your language? What was difficult to use?” | Accessibility/support defects are not failed participants |
| Artist recognition / care / process usefulness | Three separate optional responses | “Did you know them? Did you care about the example? Was the process useful?” | Recognition is not relevance; allow different artists or none |
| School and other routes | Optional preferred/avoided route and reason | “Where would you want to learn this, if anywhere? Did any option feel less open after using the page?” | Neither school nor college is the required answer |
| Optional follow-through | Host-administered, separately agreed self-report around seven days | “Did you try the step, change it, find a barrier or choose not to?” | Report attempted/self-reported action separately; not verified attendance or causal transfer |

For private/silent work, use participant-reported tried/completed/declined categories; do not demand proof. Keep observed and self-reported categories separate. These questions are formative and unvalidated. A positive connection is not automatically a helpful experience.

## Criticism and minimum-data controls

Ask what felt imposed, irrelevant, stereotyped or confusing; what would make someone leave; whether a different task or direct human help would be preferable. Ask whether they would use it without an adult request. Nothing changing is an acceptable result.

No names in analysis notes, exact birthdates, charges, immigration status, case histories, addresses, trauma or copied creative work. Host reviews redaction, storage, access and small-cell disclosure. Proposed default: no audio/video; no researcher-held participant contacts; host handles any agreed follow-up. Agree retention before collection; discussion starting point is destruction of raw notes within 30 days of approved synthesis. Do not promise anonymity where it cannot be provided. Pilot permission does not authorize testimonials or marketing.

Report all offered participants, decliners, starters, completers and missing responses with denominators, without identifying small groups. Do not exclude people because they declined or could not supply a connection. Missing follow-up is missing, not success or failure. Do not sum repeated people as independent samples or claim causal before/after change. Baseline confidence can guide support; exploratory subgroup observations cannot establish treatment moderation.

## Falsification and revision decisions

Before a later authorized pilot, host and reviewers agree what concerning patterns require action. Any report of stereotype, language disrespect, pressure to abandon music, misleading promises or an unavailable support route needs a specific response; do not wait for an average rating. If usefulness rises while voluntary attempts remain absent, label interest-to-action transfer unresolved. If burden or discouragement rises, inspect task length, scaffolding and the human support route. If school-option openness falls, report it rather than claiming benefit from skill awareness.

Revise or remove harmful/confusing content and prioritize a reliable human handoff when barriers dominate. A small formative sample cannot establish safety, efficacy or generality. A later comparison/outcome study needs separate qualified design, review and authorization; this package does not prescribe exposing minors to a potentially pressuring direct-message arm. These are pilot safeguards, not a checkpoint blocking continued local research or design.
